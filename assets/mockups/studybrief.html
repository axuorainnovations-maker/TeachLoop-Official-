Modern History | 20th Century onwards

Study Brief note
BIG IDEA

The 20th century transformed human civilization through two world wars, decolonization, Cold War ideological conflict, and technological revolution—reshaping politics, society, and global power structures in ways that directly created our modern world.

Key concepts

•
World Wars as catalysts: Industrial-scale warfare that reshaped nations, empires, and international systems
•
Decolonization movements: Former colonies gained independence, redrawing maps and challenging European dominance
•
Cold War tensions: Ideological rivalry between capitalism and communism divided the globe into competing spheres
•
Technological acceleration: Nuclear weapons, computing, space travel, and media fundamentally altered society and warfare
•
Globalization emergence: Increased trade, communication, and cultural exchange created interconnected world systems
